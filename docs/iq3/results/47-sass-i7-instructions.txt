  0f18  LDG.E.CI.U8 R27, [R18]
  0f28  LDG.E.CI.U8 R26, [R18+0x1]

The eight LDG.E.CI.U16 at 0x0ec8..0x10f0 in HEAD are the raw block's code bytes, read as
16-bit halves because the raw form is only 2-byte aligned (2 + b*18).  In NEW that whole run is
gone and a single LDG.E.CI.128 sits at 0x0ee8 - the same place in the program, the same region
of the row.  The two LDG.E.CI.U8 that remain in both are read_scale reading the block's fp16
scale byte by byte; they are NOT on the codes path and the planar layout does not move them.

WHAT IS PROVEN DIRECTLY, AND WHAT IS INFERRED.  For batch (f32) the position above is the proof:
the eight U16 are gone and one LDG.128 is at 0x0ee8, inside the same address window.  For batch (f16)
and MT the same placement is inferred from the counts: their LDG.128 totals move by exactly +1
(29->30 and 30->31) while their U16 totals drop by exactly 8 (8->0), which is this same substitution
and nothing else - the activation and shared-memory staging loads are present identically in both
objects and do not change.  Marked as inference, not as a second measurement.
